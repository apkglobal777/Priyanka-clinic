LifeCare is empanelled with over 42 government and private health insurance providers (including Star Health, Max Bupa, ICICI Lombard, HDFC ERGO, Cigna, CGHS, and ECHS). Our in-house cashless TPA desk is operational 24/7 and ensures approvals are coordinated in under 30 minutes for emergency admissions.
OPD clinics operate Monday through Saturday from 08:00 AM to 08:00 PM across all 10 departments. Select senior super-specialists hold Sunday special morning clinics between 09:00 AM and 01:00 PM. Emergency and trauma consultations operate 24 hours every day.
In an emergency, patients are admitted directly through the Trauma Bay with zero initial paperwork or upfront delay. Clinical stabilization takes immediate precedence. The patient’s family or accompanying party can complete registration at the dedicated Emergency Reception within 2 hours of arrival.
For general in-patient rooms, visiting hours are 04:30 PM – 07:30 PM daily (one visitor per patient at a time to maintain clinical rest). For Intensive Care Units (ICU/CCU/NICU), visiting is strictly scheduled between 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM and 05:00 PM – 06:00 PM with sanitized protective gowning.
All diagnostic reports (blood tests, MRI scans, CT scans, and discharge summaries) are instantly uploaded to our secure Patient Portal. You will receive an encrypted download link via SMS as soon as the pathologist or radiologist signs off on the test.
